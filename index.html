self-deciding drones

Every Spatial Defence aircraft carries its intelligence on board. When the link is jammed and GPS is denied, the mission continues — the aircraft classifies targets, weighs options, and manoeuvres on its own, in milliseconds.

Trained on thousands of simulated engagements, the onboard AI makes our systems close to unbeatable in real combat conditions — while the final, critical decision always remains with a human operator.
